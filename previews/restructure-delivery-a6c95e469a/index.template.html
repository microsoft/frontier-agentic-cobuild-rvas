From idea to Azure, with an agent at your side.

Nine steps in three bands. Agents draft, build, and check. People make the decisions at each gate.

  1. 00SetupCopilot and the plugin

  2. Agree what to build

    1. 01DiscoverAzure Solution Architect You approve: discovery
    2. 02ArchitectArchitecture and cost You approve: architecture
    3. 03WireframeWireframer You approve: journey
    4. 04Task planGitHub Spec Kit You approve: ready to delegate

  3. Delegate the build

    1. 05BuildSpec Kit implement
    2. 06DesignImpeccable
    3. 07DeployAzure, Microsoft Foundry You approve: resources

  4. Verify the result

    1. 08ReviewCode and security review You approve: findings

Agents never approve a gate. Nothing is provisioned until the subscription owner approves the resource list. Walk through the path.

Why agreement comes first.

When agents write the code, the expensive mistake is building the wrong thing. Prose lets two readers agree with every sentence and still picture different systems. Diagrams and prototypes don't. The talk behind this practice explains why, with a worked public-sector example.
