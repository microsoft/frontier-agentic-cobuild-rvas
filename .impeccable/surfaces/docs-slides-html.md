# Customer discussion slides

Mode: Read / Operate

Audience: customer facilitators, technical advisers, and customer decision-makers.

Job: guide a scenario discussion one decision at a time, keep the evidence gate visible, and let the
facilitator return to the scenario or download the same deck as PDF or PowerPoint.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** This is a facilitator cockpit, not a scroll of miniature pages. One decision holds the
center while the route and facilitation cue stay visible. It refuses the generic slide-gallery
pattern where every page competes for attention.

**OWN-WORLD:** Extend the Guided Foundry Atlas. Use RVAP paper, Deep Navy gateway slides, crisp blue
signals, fine route lines, and quiet compass geometry. Controls stay squared and practical. Type is
Outfit for display and Inter for working copy, shared by the browser and generated exports.

**STORY:** The facilitator frames the outcome, discusses constraints, compares viable choices, and
sets the proof required to move on. The group can always see where it is in the route. The close
turns unresolved questions into named follow-up work.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Desktop uses three zones: a numbered route on the left, one 16:9 slide in the
center, and a facilitation cue on the right. Scenario return and downloads sit in the toolbar.
Mobile keeps the return and downloads visible, pins a compact facilitation cue above the slide, and
lets wide comparison tables scroll without compressing their columns.

**FORM:** Guided facilitator cockpit, selected directly from the user's confirmed guided-view brief.
Concept seed `fab78465` was recorded after the brief; the user-pinned structure outranks its dealt
challengers. The signature interaction is keyboard or button movement through deep-linkable
Discuss, Decide, and Prove states while the route marker advances.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved decisions: none for this pass.
